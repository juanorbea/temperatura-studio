# temperatura-studio

Turns a written Spanish narration into a narrated video for the YouTube channel
@temperaturacero: Spanish voiceover, a sequence of AI-generated images in one
consistent look, and simple code-made graphics (titles, charts, timelines),
stitched together with ffmpeg.
